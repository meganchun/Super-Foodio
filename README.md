# Super Foodio

My game takes inspiration from classic Super Mario to offer a fun and engaging experience where you can navigate through levels, collect stars, and face various challenges. Enjoy the freedom to personalize your gameplay with different characters and backgrounds, and see each players highscore!

## Features

- **Level Progression:** Navigate through a series of new and exciting levels, each with unique challenges.
- **Star Collection:** Gather stars throughout the levels to unlock achievements and rewards.
- **Character Selection:** Choose from a variety of characters, each with distinct abilities.
- **Background Customization:** Select from different board themes to personalize your game environment.
- **Music and Sound Effects:** Enjoy immersive music and engaging sound effects throughout your gameplay.
- **Highscore Tracking:** Save your highscore along with player initials to an external text file. 

## Gameplay

- **Navigating Levels:** Move through different levels, each designed with unique challenges and obstacles.
- **Collecting Stars:** Gather stars scattered throughout the levels to earn rewards and unlock new features.
- **Overcoming Challenges:** Face various obstacles and enemies as you advance through the game.

## Technical Details

- Java, Java Swing GUI, arrays, arraylists, OOP
