# Super Foodio

My game takes inspiration from classic Super Mario to offer a fun and engaging experience where you can navigate through levels, collect stars, and face various challenges. Enjoy the freedom to personalize your gameplay with different characters and backgrounds, and retain your progress as you log in and out.

## Features

- **Level Progression:** Advance through multiple levels, each with unique challenges and obstacles.
- **Star Collection:** Gather stars as you progress to unlock achievements and rewards.
- **Character Selection:** Choose from a variety of characters, each with unique abilities.
- **Background Customization:** Select different backgrounds to enhance your gameplay experience.
- **Progress Retention:** Save your progress and continue where you left off by logging in.

## Gameplay

- **Navigating Levels:** Move through different levels, each designed with unique challenges and obstacles.
- **Collecting Stars:** Gather stars scattered throughout the levels to earn rewards and unlock new features.
- **Overcoming Challenges:** Face various obstacles and enemies as you advance through the game.
- **Saving Progress:** Your progress is automatically saved, allowing you to continue from where you left off upon re-login.

## Technical Details

- Java, Java Swing GUI, arrays, arraylists, OOP
